01 · Featured Case Study

Asiana
Airlines

In 2006, Asiana Airlines introduced a new identity system within the Kumho Asiana Group’s unified CI framework. SodiumPartners’ public archive documents the airline-specific work across class naming, brand identity and technical drawing.

The system paired the group Wing mark with Asiana’s established saekdong heritage and extended it into aircraft, airports, ticketing, lounges, vehicles and other touchpoints. Aircraft-livery design and technical consulting are credited by SodiumPartners to Joseph McGuire Associates, San Francisco.

2006 · Seoul / Incheon

Identity idea

Energy of Saekdong.

SodiumPartners describes the visual concept as a reinterpretation of Asiana’s existing “saekdong wings” and warm-grey assets into a more contemporary, dynamic system. Overlapping curves carry the color and rhythm of traditional Korean saekdong into a modern airline context.

Asiana’s own CI page still names the graphic motif “ENERGY OF SAEKDONG” and describes it as a modern development of traditional Korean color culture.

The airline application system moved from a mark to an environment: aircraft, signage, service classes, printed matter and digital templates.
One identity.
Many moments.
2006

From aircraft to the airport.

On 26 October 2006, Asiana unveiled Boeing 777 HL7597 in the new livery at Incheon International Airport. Contemporary reporting says the aircraft entered service the next day on the Incheon–Narita OZ104 route. The identity was rolled into sales and airport signage, ticket covers, Asiana Club cards and advertising; Economy Class was renamed Travel Class.

Launch facts cross-checked against Asiana-related 2006 reporting and the airline’s later corporate-history material.

Public sources reviewed do not itemize JaeYoun Park’s individual responsibilities on this case, so it is presented at studio/team level.
